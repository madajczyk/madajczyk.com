A gentle reminder that not all incidents require a Root Cause Analysis.

> **Mom:** The internet isn't working. I tried to check the weather.
>
> **Me:** Oh? Interesting. Let me see.
>
> **Mom:** Can you fix it?
>
> **Me:** Well, technically the issue may be related to VRRP state transitions resulting from interface renumbering after a storage migration.
>
> **Mom:** ...
>
> **Me:** The floating virtual IP appears to have become marooned.
>
> **Mom:** I wanted to check the weather.
>
> **Me:** Okay, so. The DNS control plane has experienced a minor disruption.
>
> **Mom:** I wanted to check the weather.
>
> **Me:** Well, looks like there's a possibility that the `enp6s0` device no longer exists.
>
> **Mom:** I wanted. To check. The weather.

Blameless postmortem? Good luck.
